Ways to Get Universal Gold Heads

Reference directory of major events offering Universal Gold Heads & commander sculptures.

Gem-Spending & Premium Events

More Than Gems (MTG)

Occurs every 4 to 6 weeks over 2 days. Spending 25,000 gems per day (50k gems total) yields 26 Universal Gold Heads (13 per day: 5 at 7k tier, 8 at 25k tier).

7k Gem Seasonal Events (Holiday Board)

Major holidays (Anniversary, Spring Festival, Halloween). Unlocking the 7,000 gem path and clearing Level 25 gives 35 Gold Heads while fully refunding the 7,000 gems.

Wheel of Fortune

Spinning gives featured commander heads directly plus guaranteed milestone chests at 10, 25, 45, 70, and 100 spins.

Card King

Use gems to selectively flip cards and unlock past legendary commanders without waiting for Wheel rotations.

Recharge Rewards (Gem Rush)

Purchasing 2,500 gems daily across 5 consecutive event days unlocks progression tiers rewarding up to 30–40 Gold Heads.

Commander Reset & Holiday Shops

Reset retired commanders (100 gems per head refunded up to 69k gems) or purchase remaining festival shop currencies.

Recurring In-Game & Competitive Events

Ark of Osiris (AOO)

Every two weeks. Hitting 10,000 personal score yields 10 Gold Heads for a win and 5 Gold Heads for a loss.

Champions of Olympia

Weekly 5v5 battle mode. Redeem up to 2 Legendary Sculptures per week from the Olympia shop (260 heads/year).

The Golden Kingdom

Bi-weekly dungeon mode. Clearing floors and defeating guardians grants guaranteed good materials from clear chests.

Mightiest Governor & Power Up Events

Kingdom-wide competitions (Game of Power / Lord of War). Top placements grant up to 20 Universal or specialized heads.
