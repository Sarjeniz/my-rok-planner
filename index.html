Complete Gold Head Events Directory

Full inventory of in-game events yielding Universal Gold Heads and commander sculptures.

Gem-Spending & Premium Events

More Than Gems (MTG)

Occurs every 4 to 6 weeks over 2 days. Spending 25,000 gems per day (50k gems total) yields 26 Universal Gold Heads (13 per day: 5 at the 7k tier, 8 at the 25k tier).

7k Gem Seasonal Events (Holiday Board)

Major holidays (Anniversary, Spring Festival, Halloween). Unlocking the 7,000 gem path and reaching Level 25 yields 35 Gold Heads while fully refunding your 7,000 gems.

Wheel of Fortune

Spinning gives featured commander heads directly plus guaranteed milestone chests (10, 25, 45, 70, and 100 spins) that grant Universal and Commander heads.

Card King

Allows using gems to selectively unlock past legendary commanders without relying entirely on Wheel rotations or Golden Chests.

Recharge Rewards (Gem Rush)

Purchasing ~2,500 gems daily across 5 consecutive event days unlocks progression tiers rewarding up to 30–40 Universal Gold Heads.

Holiday Exchange Shops & Commander Reset

Buy leftover festival currencies with gems (~1k gem equivalent per head) or reset unused expertised commanders (100 gems per sculpture refunded up to 69k gems).

Recurring In-Game & Competitive Events

Ark of Osiris (AOO)

Every two weeks. Hitting 10,000 personal points yields 10 Gold Heads for a team win and 5 Gold Heads for a loss.

Champions of Olympia

Weekly 5v5 battle mode. Ranking up awards shop coins to redeem up to 5 Legendary Sculptures per week (260 heads/year).

The Golden Kingdom

Bi-weekly dungeon crawl mode. Clearing Floor 4 and final bosses awards guaranteed Universal Sculptures from clear chests.

Mightiest Governor (MGE) & 20-Head Power Ups

Kingdom-wide 6-stage events or Lord of War/Game of Power. High rankings grant up to 20 Universal or specialized featured commander heads.
